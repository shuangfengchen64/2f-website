FAQ

Common Questions

How much does a bathroom renovation cost?▼
A standard bathroom renovation in Melbourne typically ranges from ,000 to ,000+, depending on size, fixtures and finishes. We provide a detailed, itemised quote after a free site visit — no hidden costs.
Do I need permits for renovations?▼
Most internal renovations (bathrooms, kitchens, laundries) don't require a permit. Extensions, structural changes and new builds do — we guide you through the planning and permit process.
How long does a renovation take?▼
A bathroom renovation usually takes 2-3 weeks. A kitchen 2-4 weeks. Extensions vary from 6-12 weeks. We give you a clear timeline before work starts.
Are you licensed and insured?▼
Yes. 2F PTY LTD is a registered Victorian building company with full public liability insurance. All work is carried out by qualified tradespeople under a formal contract.
Do you provide a warranty?▼
Yes. All workmanship is backed by our own warranty, and roof materials come with manufacturer warranties. We stand behind our work long after handover.
